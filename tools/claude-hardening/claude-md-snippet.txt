
Dead ends: never stall a whole session on one blocked step. Long-running or interactive commands (ollama serve/pull, TUIs, editors, REPLs, docker pulls) go in the background with run_in_background and a log file, never the foreground. A command that needs a password (sudo) or a terminal is the user's to run with `!`; ask once, precisely, and move on. If a permission denial or the auto-mode classifier blocks an action, do not retry variants or route around it with another tool: record it as a `decision` row in docs/open-problems.md (owner: you), send a PushNotification naming the exact action and why it is needed, and continue with every branch that does not depend on it. Batch what is blocked and report it together at the end. Go is at ~/.local/go/bin/go and is not on PATH: prefer `make` targets, or export PATH="$HOME/.local/go/bin:$PATH" GOTOOLCHAIN=local in the same command.
