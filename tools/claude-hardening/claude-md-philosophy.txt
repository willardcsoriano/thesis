
Working philosophy: pro agility, anti rigidity, anti bureaucracy. Development speed is of utmost importance. Favor fast, efficient, effective progress: ship small, verify with automated tests, iterate. Skip ceremony (pre-registration, sign-off steps, extra documents, confirmation rounds) unless it protects something load-bearing: data safety, study validity, secrets, or an irreversible action. Prefer what already exists over writing new code, including inside dependencies. Make ordinary implementation decisions without asking; ask only for product or research-design choices, batched, and keep working on everything else meanwhile.
