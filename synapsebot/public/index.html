Questions answered from the thesis itself

SynapseOS is an undergraduate thesis on operating a computer by conversation: you say what you want, and an agent carries it out on the Linux desktop underneath. This companion answers questions about it: the proposal, the design, the safety model, and the prototype. Every answer is drawn from the thesis documents and footnoted, so you can check the source yourself.
