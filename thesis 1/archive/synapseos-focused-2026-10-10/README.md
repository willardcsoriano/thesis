## Overview

This directory is a frozen snapshot of the proposal (Chapters 1–3) as it stood on 2026-10-10, the last version framed around SynapseOS itself: the title was "SynapseOS: Recoverability Analysis of Language-Model-Generated Shell Commands in a Conversational Interface Layer", building and evaluating the interface were objectives 4 and 5, and the user study was a primary evaluation. On 2026-10-12 the adviser asked for the recoverability analysis to become the research contribution and SynapseOS its experimental platform, and the live proposal in `thesis 1/consolidated/` was restructured accordingly. This copy keeps the interface-focused version reachable for later use: the interface, its design rationale, and the user study as a co-equal contribution are material for a future paper or for Thesis 2 and 3. Nothing here should be edited.
