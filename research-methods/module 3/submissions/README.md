## Overview

This directory is intentionally empty. Chapter 2 (Review of Related Literature) and Chapter 3 (Methodology) were drafted here as `summative-assessment-2.html` and `summative-assessment-3.1.html`, but neither was ever individually submitted through the course portal — only Chapter 1 (`research-methods/module 2/submissions/`) and the consolidated Chapters 1-3 monograph (`research-methods/consolidated/`) were actually submitted. Keeping two editable copies of the same content (the standalone drafts and the consolidated document) risked drift between them, so the standalone drafts were deleted and the consolidated document is now the sole source of truth for Chapter 2 and Chapter 3 content, maintained by hand rather than generated from separate chapter files.
